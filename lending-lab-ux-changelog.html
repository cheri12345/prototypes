Lending Lab — UX Change Log

Overall: The foundation is strong. This is a refinement pass focused on clarity, hierarchy, trust and reducing friction — not a major redesign.

Change Why
Apply CHELCIE brand colours + typography Makes Lending Lab feel connected to the CHELCIE ecosystem and less generic/AI-generated.
Remove duplicate “Lending Lab / Powered by CHELCIE” Reduces clutter and gives the value proposition more space.
Use sentence case, not ALL CAPS Improves readability and feels more aligned with CHELCIE’s brand.
Remove “Live” badge “Live” is ambiguous and creates unnecessary visual competition.
Replace “Live” with “Decision in minutes” Communicates a meaningful user benefit rather than a status.
Remove green dot from “Indicative terms” A green status dot suggests something is live/available, which is misleading here.
Link “Powered by CHELCIE” to CHELCIE Gives users a clear route back to the parent brand.
Reduce primary CTAs Repeating “Start Funding” everywhere dilutes its impact.
Hero: keep one strong “Start Funding” CTA under Capital Access Creates a clear relationship between the proposition and the action.
Remove left-side Start Funding button Avoids competing CTAs in the hero.
Navigation: retain Start Funding while scrolling Keeps the action accessible without repeatedly dominating the page.
Small Loans section: make Start Funding a text link This section is primarily for exploring needs/terms; the CTA doesn’t need to compete visually.
Review/remove “See How It Works” hero CTA The How It Works content is immediately below, so the extra CTA may be unnecessary.
Make key metrics more prominent Users should understand the core proof points while scanning the page.
Make Steps 1–4 visually larger Makes the four-step process immediately scannable.
Remove hover states from non-clickable elements Avoids implying interactions that don’t exist.
Remove unnecessary “Loans” subtitles Lets the stronger headline do the work and reduces visual noise.
Align the content cards Small visual refinement for consistency and polish.
Keep the funding simulator Gives users a tangible way to understand their potential funding needs.
Lead with “Does not affect your business credit” Addresses a key concern users may have before starting a funding journey.
Update the CHELCIE logo in the application widget Ensures the application experience feels authentically CHELCIE-branded.
Keep the current application flow for now It achieves the immediate “get it live” goal; deeper flow improvements can come later.
Make Eligibility more prominent Users need to quickly understand whether Lending Lab is relevant to them.
Put Eligibility before Start Funding Prevents users from investing time in an application only to discover they aren’t eligible.
Journey: Eligibility → Start Funding → Application Creates a clearer, more supportive progression into the lending flow.
Keep “Free to check”, “10 minutes to apply” & “Clear terms” Strong reassurance points that reduce perceived risk and friction.
Keep “Capital coordination, minus the friction” Strong, simple articulation of the product proposition.

The key principle

Make the important things easy to find, without making everything visually loud.

We want users to be able to understand → check eligibility → start funding without unnecessary clutter, competing CTAs or misleading interactions.
